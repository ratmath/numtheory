A discriminant

The class group counts forms ax2+bxy+cy2ax^2 + bxy + cy^2ax2+bxy+cy2 with b2−4ac=Db^2 - 4ac = Db2−4ac=D up to change of variables. For a positive non-square D the Pell line lists the first solutions of x2−Dy2=Nx^2 - Dy^2 = Nx2−Dy2=N; for D ≡ 2, 3 mod 4 the forms of discriminant 4D are the relevant ones.

A number field

The coefficients of an irreducible polynomial, constant term first: -7, 0, 0, 1 is x3−7x^3 - 7x3−7. The class group and the units are proven; see Class groups for how.
